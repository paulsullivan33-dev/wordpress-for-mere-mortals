A draft, made in WP-CLI, and the three places it must and must not appear:

$ wp post create --post_title="Harvest Supper Tickets Go On Sale Monday" \
    --post_content="Tickets for the harvest supper go on sale ..." \
    --post_status=draft --porcelain
6

$ curl -s http://127.0.0.1:18080/ | grep -c "Harvest Supper"
0
$ curl -s "http://127.0.0.1:18080/wp-json/wp/v2/posts?per_page=50" | grep -c "Harvest Supper"
0
$ wp post list --post_status=draft --fields=ID,post_title,post_status
ID	post_title	post_status
6	Harvest Supper Tickets Go On Sale Monday	draft

Invisible to readers and to the public API; present where the editor works.
